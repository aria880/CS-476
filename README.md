# SportsLink

A platform connecting Canadian student amature athletes with university and junior programs. The app would allow athletes to post content like highlight links, statistics, or accomplishments on their profiles. While coaches or sporting organizations can post team information such as wins/losses, program benefits, or day in the life content. All users would be able to utilize search functions to find specific teams or players, add, tag and message friends, or post public events for other users to interact with.

We believe this project would be needed because there are many people who play sports, but not a lot of opportunities to gather in one place to share information with potential coaches/recruiters and our app would allow athletes to build a reputation based on their athletic skills. It will also make this information visible to other stakeholders such as schools, clubs, brands to view and factor into recruiting decisions.
